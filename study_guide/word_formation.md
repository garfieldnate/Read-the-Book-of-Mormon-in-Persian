## Note on stress

Persian stress falls on the **last syllable** of a content word. Every derivational suffix on this page is added at the end of the base, so the suffix becomes the new last syllable and takes the stress:

| Base | → | Derived form | Stress shift |
|------|---|-------------|--------------|
| `خوب` *xūb* | + ـی | `خوبی` *xūb-**ī*** | onto the suffix |
| `زنده` *zende* | + ـگی | `زندگی` *zendeg-**ī*** | onto the suffix |
| `مرد` *mard* | + ـانه | `مردانه` *mardān-**e*** | onto the suffix |
| `نگهدار` *negah-dār* | + ـی | `نگهداری` *negahdār-**ī*** | suffix added after already-final stress |

The practical takeaway: when you hear an unfamiliar word with stress late in the word, consider whether a suffix has been added to a base you already know.

## Prefixes

### بی‌- (*bī-*) — "without, lacking X"

The most common Persian negation prefix. Attaches to **nouns** to produce adjectives meaning "without X" or "lacking X." Fully productive — you can add بی‌- to virtually any noun.

| Base | | بی‌- + | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `گناه` *gonāh* "sin" | noun | `بی‌گناه` *bī-go-**nāh*** | "sinless, innocent" |
| `رحم` *rahm* "mercy" | noun | `بی‌رحم` *bī-**rahm*** | "merciless, cruel" |
| `خرد` *xerad* "wisdom" | noun | `بی‌خرد` *bī-xe-**rad*** | "witless, foolish" |
| `شمار` *šomār* "count, number" | noun | `بی‌شمار` *bī-šo-**mār*** | "innumerable, countless" |
| `ایمان` *imān* "faith" | noun | `بی‌ایمان` *bī-i-**mān*** | "faithless, unbelieving" |
| `خانمان` *xānamān* "home, hearth" | noun | `بی‌خانمان` *bī-xāna-**mān*** | "homeless" |

**Stress:** the prefix is unstressed; stress falls on the last syllable of the noun base.

### نا‌- (*nā-*) — "un-, non-, in-"

The second Persian negation prefix. Where بی‌- attaches to nouns ("without X"), نا‌- typically attaches to **adjectives and participial forms** ("un-[adj]"), though the boundary is not rigid and some words accept either prefix.

| Base | | نا‌- + | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `پاک` *pāk* "pure, clean" | adj | `ناپاک` *nā-**pāk*** | "impure, unclean" |
| `درست` *dorost* "correct, right" | adj | `نادرست` *nā-do-**rost*** | "incorrect, wrong" |
| `توان` *tavān* "power, ability" | noun | `ناتوان` *nā-ta-**vān*** | "powerless, weak" |
| `کام` *kām* "wish, desire" | noun | `ناکام` *nā-**kām*** | "unfulfilled, frustrated" |
| `بود` *bud* past stem of "to be" | verbal element | `نابود` *nā-**bud*** | "non-existent, annihilated" |
| `بکار` *be-kār* "useful, serviceable" | adj | `نابکار` *nā-be-**kār*** | "wicked, worthless" |

**Stress:** the prefix is unstressed; stress falls on the last syllable of the base.

**بی‌- vs نا‌-:** A few words take either prefix with near-identical meaning (e.g., `بی‌امید` and `ناامید` are both "hopeless"), but most take only one. When in doubt: if the base is a standalone noun, reach for بی‌-; if it is an adjective or participle, reach for نا‌-.

### هم‌- (*ham-*) — "co-, fellow-, together"

Attaches to nouns of role, activity, or quality to mean "one who shares X with another" or "characterized by sharing." Also functions as a preposition-like word meaning "together with, along with."

| Base | | هم‌- + | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `راه` *rāh* "road, way" | noun | `همراه` *ham-**rāh*** | "companion; together with" |
| `سفر` *safar* "journey" | noun | `همسفر` *ham-sa-**far*** | "fellow traveler" |
| `کار` *kār* "work" | noun | `همکار` *ham-**kār*** | "colleague, coworker" |
| `درد` *dard* "pain, grief" | noun | `همدردی` *ham-dar-**dī*** | "sympathy" (+ abstract ـی) |
| `صدا` *sedā* "voice" | noun | `همصدا` *ham-se-**dā*** | "in unison, of one voice" |
| `نظر` *nazar* "view, opinion" | noun | `هم‌نظر` *ham-na-**zar*** | "of the same opinion, agreeing" |

**Stress:** prefix is unstressed; stress on the last syllable of the base.

## The abstract-noun suffix ـی / ـگی (*-ī* / *-gī*)

Persian has a **fully productive** suffix **-ī** (ـی) that converts adjectives, agent nouns, and compound descriptors into abstract nouns meaning "the quality, state, or practice of X." It is one of the most common derivational patterns in the language, and the source of a large number of the nouns you will encounter in this text. Whenever you see a noun ending in ـی that can be decomposed as `[adjective or agent noun] + ی`, this pattern is almost certainly at work — even for words you have never seen before.

### From adjectives

| Base | | + ـی | |
|------|-|------|--|
| `خوب` *xūb* "good" | adj | `خوبی` *xūb-**ī*** | "goodness" |
| `آگاه` *āgāh* "aware, informed" | adj | `آگاهی` *āgāh-**ī*** | "awareness, knowledge" |
| `نیک` *nīk* "good, virtuous" | adj | `نیکی` *nīk-**ī*** | "virtue, goodness" |
| `دارا` *dārā* "having, wealthy" | adj | `دارایی` *dārāy-**ī*** | "possessions, wealth" |
| `خیره سر` *xīre-sar* "stubborn" | compound adj | `خیره سری` *xīre-sar-**ī*** | "stubbornness" |
| `سخت دل` *saxt-del* "hard-hearted" | compound adj | `سخت دلی` *saxt-del-**ī*** | "hard-heartedness" |

### From agent nouns

Agent nouns — words formed with ـدار *-dār*, ـگر *-gar*, ـکار *-kār*, ـگزار *-gozār*, and related suffixes that denote "one who does/has X" (see [Present-stem compounding](#present-stem-compounding) below) — take ـی to mean "the practice or condition of being that kind of person":

| Base | | + ـی | |
|------|-|------|--|
| `نگهدار` *negah-dār* "guardian, keeper" | agent noun | `نگهداری` *negahdār-**ī*** | "keeping, preservation, maintenance" |
| `پرهیزکار` *parhīz-kār* "pious, abstinent" | agent noun | `پرهیزکاری` *parhīzkār-**ī*** | "piety, righteousness" |
| `سپاسگزار` *sepāsgozār* "grateful" | agent noun | `سپاسگزاری` *sepāsgozār-**ī*** | "gratitude, thanksgiving" |
| `فرمانروا` *farmān-ravā* "ruler" | agent noun | `فرمانروایی` *farmānravāy-**ī*** | "rule, sovereignty" |
| `رؤیانگر` *roʾyānegar* "visionary" | agent noun | `رؤیانگری` *roʾyānegar-**ī*** | "the state of being a visionary" |

### The ـگی allomorph after silent ـه

When the base ends in a **silent ه** (pronounced *-e* but written ه), the suffix takes the form **-gī** (ـگی) — the *g* is a linking consonant that prevents two vowels from colliding:

| Base | | + ـگی | |
|------|-|-------|--|
| `زنده` *zende* "alive" | adj | `زندگی` *zendeg-**ī*** | "life" |
| `ساده` *sāde* "simple, plain" | adj | `سادگی` *sādeg-**ī*** | "simplicity" |
| `شرمنده` *šarmande* "ashamed" | adj | `شرمندگی` *šarmandeg-**ī*** | "shame, embarrassment" |
| `آکنده` *ākande* "filled" | adj | `آکندگی` *ākandeg-**ī*** | "fullness" |

### Three uses of ـی — don't confuse them

The letter ی produces three completely different grammatical effects depending on what it attaches to and where it sits in the sentence:

| Use | What it attaches to | Result | Example |
|-----|---------------------|--------|---------|
| **Abstract noun** (this section) | adjective or agent noun | a new noun | `خوب` → `خوبی` "goodness" |
| **Indefinite marker** | any noun | "a, some, a certain" | `مرد` → `مردی` "a man" |
| **Relativizer** | noun before a `که` clause | identifies the antecedent | `خدایی که` "the God who…" |

The abstract-noun use is recognizable because the result is itself a noun that stands alone (it is not followed by که, and it does not mean "a [noun]"). When in doubt, ask: does the ـی-word name a quality, state, or practice? If so, it is the abstract suffix.

## The adverb/adjective suffix ـانه (*-āne*)

Persian has a **fully productive** suffix **-āne** (ـانه) that attaches to nouns and adjectives to create words meaning "in the manner of X" or "befitting X." The resulting form can function as either an **adjective** (modifying a noun before it) or an **adverb** (modifying a verb after it) — context determines which reading applies.

| Base | | + ـانه | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `خرد` *xerad* "wisdom, reason" | noun | `خردانه` *xerad-ān-**e*** | "wisely, wise" |
| `مرد` *mard* "man" | noun | `مردانه` *mard-ān-**e*** | "manly, courageously" |
| `دوست` *dust* "friend" | noun | `دوستانه` *dust-ān-**e*** | "friendly, in a friendly manner" |
| `دشمن` *došman* "enemy" | noun | `دشمنانه` *došman-ān-**e*** | "hostile, in a hostile manner" |
| `سال` *sāl* "year" | noun | `سالانه` *sāl-ān-**e*** | "annual, annually" |
| `عاقل` *ʿāqel* "wise, rational" | adj | `عاقلانه` *ʿāqel-ān-**e*** | "sensibly, prudently" |
| `جاهل` *jāhel* "ignorant" | adj | `جاهلانه` *jāhel-ān-**e*** | "ignorantly, foolishly" |

### With بی‌ compounds

The suffix attaches freely to **بی‌-** ("without") compounds, producing adverbs that mean "in the manner of one who lacks X":

| Base | | + ـانه | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `بی خرد` *bī-xerad* "lacking wisdom, senseless" | بی‌ compound | `بی خردانه` *bī-xerad-ān-**e*** | "foolishly, senselessly" |
| `بی رحم` *bī-rahm* "merciless" | بی‌ compound | `بی رحمانه` *bī-rahm-ān-**e*** | "mercilessly" |
| `بی عدالت` *bī-ʿadālat* "unjust" | بی‌ compound | `بی عدالتانه` *bī-ʿadālat-ān-**e*** | "unjustly" |

### The ـگانه allomorph after silent ـه

Parallel to the -ī/-gī pattern, when the base ends in a silent ه (pronounced *-e*), the suffix takes the form **-gāne** (ـگانه):

| Base | | + ـگانه | |
|------|-|---------|--|
| `بچه` *bačče* "child" | noun | `بچگانه` *bačče-gān-**e*** | "childish, childishly" |
| `دسته` *daste* "group" | noun | `دستگانه` *dast-gān-**e*** | "collectively" |

### Function: adjective and adverb simultaneously

Unlike English, where "friendly" (adj) and "in a friendly manner" (adv) are distinct forms, a single ـانه word covers both roles. Word order and context tell you which is meant:

| Persian | Reading | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|
| `رفتار دوستانه` *raftār-e dust-āne* | adjective | "a friendly manner" (modifies رفتار) |
| `دوستانه رفتار کرد` *dust-āne raftār kard* | adverb | "he behaved in a friendly way" (modifies رفتار کرد) |

## Quality and attribute suffixes

### ـمند (*-mand*) — "endowed with, possessing X"

Attaches to nouns of knowledge, skill, or abstract quality to produce adjectives and agent nouns meaning "one endowed with X." **Highly productive** with abstract nouns.

| Base | | + ـمند | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `دانش` *dāneš* "knowledge" | noun | `دانشمند` *dāneš-**mand*** | "scholar, learned person" |
| `هنر` *honar* "art, skill" | noun | `هنرمند` *honar-**mand*** | "artist, skilled person" |
| `توان` *tavān* "power, ability" | noun | `توانمند` *tavān-**mand*** | "capable, powerful" |
| `ارزش` *arzeš* "value, worth" | noun | `ارزشمند` *arzeš-**mand*** | "valuable, worthwhile" |
| `خرد` *xerad* "wisdom" | noun | `خردمند` *xerad-**mand*** | "wise, sage" |
| `نیاز` *niyāz* "need" | noun | `نیازمند` *niyāz-**mand*** | "needy, in need" |

### ـناک (*-nāk*) — "full of, fraught with X"

Attaches to nouns of negative or intense emotion/quality to produce adjectives meaning "characterized by X" or "causing X." **Highly productive**, especially for words describing danger, pain, and fear.

| Base | | + ـناک | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `خطر` *xatar* "danger" | noun | `خطرناک` *xatar-**nāk*** | "dangerous" |
| `درد` *dard* "pain, grief" | noun | `دردناک` *dard-**nāk*** | "painful, grievous" |
| `ترس` *tars* "fear" | noun | `ترسناک` *tars-**nāk*** | "frightening, scary" |
| `شرم` *šarm* "shame" | noun | `شرمناک` *šarm-**nāk*** | "shameful, disgraceful" |
| `هول` *howl* "terror, dread" | noun | `هولناک` *howl-**nāk*** | "terrifying, dreadful" |
| `غم` *ɣam* "sorrow, grief" | noun | `غمناک` *ɣam-**nāk*** | "sorrowful, mournful" |

### ـور (*-var*) — "possessing, bearing X"

A somewhat literary suffix meaning "one who has or bears X." Less freely productive than ـمند in modern Persian — mostly encountered in inherited literary words rather than new coinages — but common enough in formal and religious texts to be worth recognizing.

| Base | | + ـور | |
|------|-|-------|--|
| `نام` *nām* "name, fame" | noun | `نامور` *nām-**var*** | "famous, renowned" |
| `دانش` *dāneš* "knowledge" | noun | `دانشور` *dāneš-**var*** | "learned, scholarly" (synonym of دانشمند) |
| `بهره` *bahre* "share, benefit" | noun | `بهره‌ور` *bahre-**var*** | "productive, benefiting" |
| `زور` *zur* "force, strength" | noun | `زورآور` *zur-ā-**var*** | "strong, forceful" |

**Note:** `زورآور` can also be analyzed as زور + آور (present stem of آوردن "to bring") — "one who brings force." This overlap between ـور and the present stem آور from آوردن means the suffix can be difficult to isolate. When you see ـور and cannot identify a clear noun base, check whether آوردن (to bring/produce) might be in play.

## Present-stem compounding

Persian verbs each have a **present stem** — the bare root form visible in the present tense. It is what remains when you strip the personal ending from the he/she/it present tense form (or equivalently, strip می‌ and the ending). Unlike many other languages, the present stem is not always predictable from the infinitive and must be learned per verb:

| Infinitive | | Present stem |
|------------|--|-------------|
| `رفتن` *raftan* "to go" | → | `رو` *raw* |
| `گفتن` *goftan* "to say" | → | `گو` *gu* |
| `دیدن` *didan* "to see" | → | `بین` *bin* |
| `آوردن` *āvardan* "to bring" | → | `آور` *āvar* |
| `جستن` *jostan* "to seek" | → | `جو` *ju* |
| `سوختن` *suxtan* "to burn" | → | `سوز` *suz* |
| `نشستن` *nešastan* "to sit" | → | `نشین` *nešin* |
| `داشتن` *dāštan* "to have, hold" | → | `دار` *dār* |
| `کردن` *kardan* "to do, make" | → | `کن` *kon* / `کار` *kār* |

The present stem combines with nouns following the pattern **[noun] + [present stem]** → a compound meaning "one who does [verb] to/with [noun]," "characterized by doing [verb] with [noun]," or a place/instrument for doing [verb] to [noun]. This is one of the most productive word-formation patterns in Persian:

| Compound | | Meaning |
|----------|--|---------|
| `دانش` (knowledge) + `جو` (seek) | = `دانشجو` *dāneš-**ju*** | "student" (knowledge-seeker) |
| `دل` (heart) + `سوز` (burn) | = `دلسوز` *del-**suz*** | "compassionate" (heart-burning) |
| `نان` (bread) + `آور` (bring) | = `نان‌آور` *nān-ā-**var*** | "breadwinner" |
| `راه` (road) + `رو` (go) | = `راهرو` *rāh-**raw*** | "corridor; passerby" |
| `جهان` (world) + `بین` (see) | = `جهان‌بین` *jahān-**bin*** | "worldview; observer of the world" |
| `خانه` (house) + `نشین` (sit) | = `خانه‌نشین` *xāne-ne-**šin*** | "homebody; housebound; in seclusion" |
| `فرمان` (command) + `رو` (go/run) | = `فرمانرو` *farmān-**raw*** | "ruler" (lit. "one whose commands run") |

**Stress:** stress falls on the present-stem element (the last part): *dāneš-**ju***, *del-**suz***, *nān-ā-**var***.

The four subsections below describe the most common present stems — دار, گر, کار, گزار — which are used so frequently in this compounding pattern that they function effectively as independent suffixes.

### ـدار (*-dār*) — "one who holds or possesses X"

From the present stem of `داشتن` *dāštan* "to have, hold." Attaches to nouns to produce "one who has/holds X"; adjectivally it means "characterized by possessing X." **Highly productive** — can combine with almost any concrete or abstract noun.

| Base | | + ـدار | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `نگه` *negah* "watch, care" | noun | `نگهدار` *negah-**dār*** | "guardian, keeper" |
| `امانت` *amānat* "trust, deposit" | noun | `امانتدار` *amānat-**dār*** | "trustee, custodian" |
| `خانه` *xāne* "house" | noun | `خانه‌دار` *xāne-**dār*** | "homekeeper, householder" |
| `پول` *pul* "money" | noun | `پولدار` *pul-**dār*** | "wealthy, moneyed" |
| `سلاح` *selāh* "weapon" | noun | `سلاحدار` *selāh-**dār*** | "armed, weapon-bearer" |

### ـگر (*-gar*) — "one who works with or practices X"

Attaches to the names of materials, crafts, or activities to form occupational and agentive nouns. **Productive**, especially for crafts and social roles.

| Base | | + ـگر | |
|------|-|-------|--|
| `کار` *kār* "work, labor" | noun | `کارگر` *kār-**gar*** | "laborer, worker" |
| `آهن` *āhan* "iron" | noun | `آهنگر` *āhan-**gar*** | "blacksmith" |
| `ستم` *setam* "oppression" | noun | `ستمگر` *setam-**gar*** | "oppressor" |
| `بازی` *bāzi* "play, game" | noun | `بازیگر` *bāzi-**gar*** | "actor, player" |
| `جادو` *jādu* "magic" | noun | `جادوگر` *jādu-**gar*** | "sorcerer, wizard" |

### ـکار (*-kār*) — "one whose practice or deed is X"

From the noun `کار` *kār* "deed, act." Attaches to verbal nouns or moral-quality nouns to mean "one who does/commits X." **Moderately productive**; for some bases, both ـگر and ـکار exist (e.g., `ستمگر` and `ستمکار` are both used, with slight register differences).

| Base | | + ـکار | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `پرهیز` *parhīz* "abstinence, avoidance" | verbal noun | `پرهیزکار` *parhīz-**kār*** | "pious, self-restrained" |
| `گناه` *gonāh* "sin" | noun | `گناهکار` *gonāh-**kār*** | "sinner, guilty" |
| `خدمت` *xedmat* "service" | noun | `خدمتکار` *xedmat-**kār*** | "servant" |
| `ستم` *setam* "oppression" | noun | `ستمکار` *setam-**kār*** | "oppressor, unjust" |

### ـگزار (*-gozār*) — "one who performs or renders X"

From `گزاردن` *gozārdan* "to perform, render." Most common in religious, formal, and honorific contexts. **Less freely productive** than ـدار or ـگر; mostly encountered in inherited compounds rather than new coinages, but very frequent in literary Persian.

| Base | | + ـگزار | |
|------|-|---------|--|
| `سپاس` *sepās* "thanks, gratitude" | noun | `سپاسگزار` *sepās-**gozār*** | "grateful, thankful" |
| `نماز` *namāz* "ritual prayer" | noun | `نمازگزار` *namāz-**gozār*** | "one who prays, devout" |
| `خدمت` *xedmat* "service" | noun | `خدمتگزار` *xedmat-**gozār*** | "servant, one who serves" |
| `خراج` *xarāj* "tribute" | noun | `خراجگزار` *xarāj-**gozār*** | "one who pays tribute, vassal" |

### ـنده (*-ande*) — the participial agent

Attaches directly to the **present stem** to mean "one who does X" (a person) or "the X-ing one" (a thing or abstract). It is **fully productive** — it works with every verb — and produces some of the most common nouns and adjectives in the language. Because it requires knowing the present stem, the table below shows the stem explicitly:

| Verb | Present stem | + ـنده | |
|------|-------------|--------|--|
| `گفتن` *goftan* "to say" | `گو` *gu* | `گوینده` *guyande* | "speaker, one who says" |
| `نوشتن` *neveštan* "to write" | `نویس` *navis* | `نویسنده` *nevisande* | "writer, author" |
| `آمدن` *āmadan* "to come" | `آی` *āy* | `آینده` *āyande* | "future" (lit. "the coming one") |
| `خواندن` *xāndan* "to read/sing" | `خوان` *xān* | `خواننده` *xānande* | "reader; singer" |
| `دیدن` *didan* "to see" | `بین` *bin* | `بیننده` *binande* | "viewer, observer" |
| `فرستادن` *ferestādan* "to send" | `فرست` *ferest* | `فرستنده` *ferestande* | "sender" |
| `داشتن` *dāštan* "to have/hold" | `دار` *dār* | `دارنده` *dārandeh* | "holder, possessor" |
| `گذشتن` *gozaštan* "to pass" | `گذر` *gozar* | `گذرنده` *gozarande* | "passer-by; transient" |

Two of these have lexicalized into standard vocabulary items: `آینده` "future" (lit. "the coming one") and `گذشته` "the past" (lit. "the passed/gone"; formed from the past stem + ـه, a related participial pattern). When you encounter an unfamiliar ـنده word, the first step is to identify the present stem and look up the corresponding verb.

## Place and domain suffixes

### ـگاه (*-gāh*) — "place or time of X"

Attaches to nouns of activity or function to produce the name of a place (or occasionally a time) associated with that activity. **Productive** for institutional and architectural vocabulary.

| Base | | + ـگاه | |
|------|-|--------|--|
| `آموزش` *āmuzaš* "instruction" | noun | `آموزشگاه` *āmuzaš-**gāh*** | "school, training center" |

### ـستان (*-stān*) — "land of, place characterized by X"

Originally means "place where X is abundant or where X is the defining feature." Fully productive in the formation of place names (کردستان, گلستان) and some institutional vocabulary. Less commonly used for new everyday coinages than ـگاه.

| Base | | + ـستان | |
|------|-|---------|--|
| `گل` *gol* "flower, rose" | noun | `گلستان` *gol-**stān*** | "rose garden; garden" (also a famous poetry collection) |
| `بو` *bu* "fragrance, scent" | noun | `بوستان` *bu-**stān*** | "garden, orchard" (also a famous poetry collection) |
| `دبیر` *dabir* "scribe, learned one" | noun | `دبیرستان` *dabir-**stān*** | "high school" |
| `قبر` *qabr* "grave" | noun | `قبرستان` *qabr-**stān*** | "cemetery, graveyard" |
| `کرد` *kord* "Kurd" | proper noun | `کردستان` *kord-**stān*** | "Kurdistan" (land of the Kurds) |

**Note on place names:** ـستان is especially visible in the names of countries and regions: تاجیکستان, قزاقستان, ترکستان, etc. These all follow the same pattern — "[people/feature] + ستان" = "land of [people/feature]."
